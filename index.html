Recipe #1 Apple Pie

  • Time: 5 hours
  • Serves: 3 persons
Ingredients Quantity
pie crusts 2
Granny Smith Apples 6 to 7
sugar 3/4 cup granulated
brown sugar 1/2 cup
flour (or cornstarch) 2 to 3 tablespoons
spices (cinnamon and nutmeg) 1 and 1/2 teaspoons
acid / flavor (lemon juice) 1 tablespoon
butter 2 tablespoons
egg wash 1 egg, 1 tablespoon of water
sanding sugar 1 to 2 tablespoons

Step 1: preheat oven to 425 degrees

Step 2: peel or thinly slice your apples

Step 3: toss the filling ( the lemon juice, sugar, brown sugar, flour or cornstarch, cinnamon, nutmeg) then toss it all together thoroughly until the apple slices are evenly coated

Step 4: gently unroll one pie crsut into a 9 inch pie dish pressing it gently against the bottom and sides without stretching the dough

Step 5: pour the seasoned apple mixture into the bottom crust, pressing down lightly to minimize empty air gaps. then dot the top of the apples evenly with small butter peices

Step 6 place the second pie crust over the top of the filling. than trim any excess edge dough, tuck the top and bottom edges together, and crimp or flute the edges to seal tightly. than cut 4 small vents or slits into the center of the top crust to let steam escape

Step 7: brush the top crust with the egg wash and than sprinkle the sanding sugar

Step 8: bake it at 425 for 15 mintutes than reduce it to 350 for around 45 mintues
